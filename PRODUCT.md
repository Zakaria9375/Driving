# Ceļš

## Register
Product: a personal study tool.

## Platform
Responsive web, built with React.

## Users and purpose
The user is learning all Latvian road signs through image-first flashcards, plus a completely separate Germany collection focused on signs and driving rules that differ from Latvia. Names and explanations are in English, as requested. Original names and official sources remain available. Country progress is independent, and the Germany deck includes scenario prompts for rules without a specific sign.

## Principles
Keep the sign visible and the answer hidden until requested. Support short repeat practice, category filtering, and locally saved progress. Prioritize legible text, keyboard access, and reduced motion.
